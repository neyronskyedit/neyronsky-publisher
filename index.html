Neyronsky Publisher

A personal publishing tool by Neyronsky (@neyronsky.edit)

Neyronsky Publisher is a small desktop tool used by one creator, Yaroslav (Neyronsky), to publish his own short vertical videos to his own accounts on YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok from a single place.

How it works

  • The creator signs in to each platform once using the platform's official OAuth login.
  • He chooses a finished video file and writes a caption on his computer.
  • The tool uploads the video to his accounts through the official APIs: YouTube Data API, Instagram API with Instagram Login and TikTok Content Posting API.

It is not a public service. There are no user sign-ups, no ads and no data collection beyond what is needed to upload the creator's own videos.

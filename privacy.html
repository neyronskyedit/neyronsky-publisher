Privacy Policy

Neyronsky Publisher · Last updated: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data Neyronsky Publisher (the "App"), operated by Yaroslav, creator of @neyronsky.edit ("Neyronsky", "we"), accesses and how it is handled. The App is a personal tool used by its owner to publish his own videos to his own accounts.

1. Data the App accesses

When the owner connects an account through the platform's official OAuth login, the App receives only the permissions needed to upload videos:

  • YouTube (Google): the youtube.upload permission, used only to upload videos to the connected channel, and youtube.readonly, used only to show the name of the connected channel so the owner can confirm the right channel is selected.
  • TikTok: basic profile information (user.info.basic: account ID, display name, avatar) to confirm which account is connected, and video.upload to send videos to that account's TikTok inbox as drafts.
  • Instagram: instagram_business_basic (account ID and username) and instagram_business_content_publish to publish Reels to the connected professional account.

The App also processes the video files and captions that the owner chooses to publish.

2. How the data is used

The data is used only to upload the owner's videos and captions to his own accounts and to show the result of each upload (success or error, and a link to the post). It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics or any other purpose.

3. Where the data is stored

  • Access tokens and refresh tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer, in files that are never shared or uploaded anywhere.
  • A local log records the time, file name, platform, status and post link of each upload.
  • The App has no server and no database. We do not collect any data about other people.
  • Videos are sent directly from the owner's computer to the platform APIs. No other server stores them.
  • The page callback.html on this website only passes the sign-in result back to the App on the owner's computer. It does not store or send it anywhere else.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. Data is sent only to the platform where the owner chooses to publish (Google/YouTube, Meta/Instagram, TikTok).

5. Google API Services

The App's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The App also uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. How to revoke access and delete data

  • Google/YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • TikTok: TikTok app → Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions
  • Instagram: Instagram app → Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites

Deleting the local token files also removes the App's access on the owner's computer. To request deletion of any data, email us at the address below.

7. Cookies and tracking

The App and this website do not use cookies, analytics or trackers.

8. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly process their data.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
